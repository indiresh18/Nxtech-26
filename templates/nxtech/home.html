Official Competition Rules

Rules & Instructions

4 core guidelines for each competition. Please read carefully before registering.

Hackathon Guidelines

6-Hour Innovation Sprint

4 Points
1

Team Size & Eligibility

Exclusively for MCET First-Year students. Every team must have a fixed size of exactly 5 members (1 Team Leader + 4 Members). Inter-department teams are encouraged.

2

Problem Statement Track

Teams choose one tentative domain track during registration. Detailed challenge briefs, criteria, and mentor checkpoints will be released at kick-off.

3

6-Hour Live Development

All source code, hardware models, and presentations must be constructed during the 6-hour hackathon timeline. Pre-built or plagiarized projects will be disqualified.

4

Evaluation Criteria

Evaluated on Innovation & Feasibility (30%), Technical Implementation (30%), Practical Impact (20%), and Live Prototype Demo & Pitch (20%).

Failure Analysis Guidelines

Forensic Case Study & Defense

4 Points
1

Team Size & Eligibility

Exclusively for MCET First-Year students. Every team must have a fixed size of exactly 5 members (1 Team Leader + 4 Members) from any academic branch.

2

Open Case Investigation

No pre-assigned problem statements. Teams have total freedom to research and analyze any real-world technological, structural, or software breakdown.

3

Forensic Methodology

Analysis must demonstrate structured failure investigation tools (e.g. 5-Whys, Fishbone / Ishikawa diagram, or Fault-Tree) and sound preventive actions.

4

Jury Defense & Scoring

Judged on Investigation Depth & Evidence (35%), Failure Breakdown Precision (25%), Practicality of Proposed Fix (20%), and Presentation (20%).

⭐ INTER DEPARTMENT TEAMS WILL GET EXTRA 10 points ⭐

Cross-department squads are highly rewarded! Teams formed with members from different academic branches will receive an exclusive 10 bonus points towards their overall evaluation score.

📌 Note: Rule guidelines are tentative and will be updated closer to the competition date.
